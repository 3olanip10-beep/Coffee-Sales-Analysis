# ☕ Coffee Sales Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses coffee sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, loyalty programme effectiveness and product trends across different countries and years.

Using Microsoft Excel, I developed an interactive dashboard to explore sales patterns, understand customer behaviour and identify opportunities for improving business performance.

The dashboard brings together key performance indicators (KPIs), sales trends and customer insights to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall coffee sales performance.
- Identify the best-performing coffee products and markets.
- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour and spending patterns.
- Explore the relationship between loyalty card membership and customer spending.
- Investigate annual sales trends and seasonal product demand.
- Provide actionable business recommendations based on the analysis.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

**Microsoft Excel**

- Data analysis and reporting
- Interactive dashboard development
- Charts and visualisations
- Slicers for interactive filtering
- KPI tracking and performance monitoring

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks four key business metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Total revenue generated from coffee sales |
| Total Orders | Total number of customer orders |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | Average revenue generated per order |
| Unique Customers | Total number of individual customers |

These KPIs provide an overview of sales performance and customer activity.

## 🔍 Dashboard Analysis and Key Insights

### 1. Top 5 Customers by Sales

**Analysis:** Ranks the five highest-spending customers based on total sales revenue.

**Key Insight:** A small group of customers contributes significantly to overall sales.

**Business Implication:** High-value customers represent an important revenue segment. Retaining these customers through personalised offers and targeted engagement could support long-term revenue growth.

### 2. Best-Performing Coffee Type

**Analysis:** Compares sales revenue across different coffee varieties.

**Key Insight:** Excelsa generated the highest revenue among the coffee types analysed.

**Business Implication:** Excelsa is a particularly valuable product category. Understanding the factors driving its performance could help improve product positioning, marketing and stock management.

### 3. Country with the Highest Coffee Sales

**Analysis:** Compares total sales revenue across countries.

**Key Insight:** The United States recorded the highest coffee sales.

**Business Implication:** The United States represents the strongest market by revenue in the dataset and could be prioritised for customer retention and targeted marketing initiatives.

### 4. Total Sales per Year

**Analysis:** Examines annual sales performance to identify revenue trends.

**Key Insight:** Sales increased steadily before declining in the most recent year shown.

**Business Implication:** The decline warrants further investigation to determine whether it reflects changes in customer demand, market conditions, sales activity or incomplete reporting for the latest year.

### 5. Total Number of Orders per Year

**Analysis:** Tracks the volume of orders placed each year.

**Key Insight:** The highest number of orders was recorded in 2021.

**Business Implication:** Order activity was strongest during this period. Comparing 2021 with subsequent years could help identify changes in purchasing frequency and customer engagement.

### 6. Coffee Sales Over Time by Type

**Analysis:** Examines how sales of different coffee varieties change over time.

**Key Insight:** Coffee types exhibit different sales patterns, including seasonal variations.

**Business Implication:** Understanding these patterns can support demand forecasting, inventory planning and better timing of promotional activities.

### 7. Loyalty Card Customer Spending Over Time

**Analysis:** Explores spending patterns among customers with loyalty cards.

**Key Insight:** Loyalty card customers consistently contribute significant sales revenue.

**Business Implication:** Loyalty card holders represent an important customer segment. Further analysis comparing members and non-members would help assess whether membership is associated with higher spending and repeat purchases.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes five slicers that allow users to filter and explore sales data dynamically.

| Slicer | Function |
|---|---|
| Loyalty Card Status | Compare customers based on loyalty membership |
| Coffee Size | Analyse performance across product sizes |
| Roast Type | Explore sales by coffee roast category |
| Country | Compare performance across geographical markets |
| Order Year | Examine sales performance for specific years |

These filters make it possible to explore the dataset from different perspectives without manually changing the underlying analysis.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings, I recommend the following:

**1. Strengthen Customer Loyalty Initiatives**

Develop targeted rewards and retention strategies for existing loyalty card holders while encouraging more customers to participate.

**2. Prioritise High-Performing Coffee Products**

Use the strong performance of Excelsa to inform marketing campaigns, product positioning and inventory decisions.

**3. Maintain a Strong Presence in the United States**

Given its contribution to total revenue, the U.S. market should remain an important focus for customer engagement and sales growth.

**4. Investigate the Recent Sales Decline**

Conduct further analysis to understand the reasons behind the decline in annual sales, including checking whether the most recent year contains a complete reporting period.

**5. Introduce Customer Segmentation**

Group customers based on spending behaviour, purchasing frequency and loyalty membership to develop more personalised marketing strategies.

**6. Improve Inventory Forecasting**

Use historical demand patterns to anticipate changes in coffee sales and improve product availability.
